How it works & privacy

Wordsplice searches YouGlish, plays the referenced YouTube clips, and records the tab you select. Local speech recognition locates the words. Some phrases may be unavailable; listen back and adjust cuts before exporting.

Your microphone is never used. Only audio is saved; the screen video track is never recorded. Audio and the speech model stay on this computer. Search phrases go to YouGlish, and YouTube receives player requests. Use recordings you have permission to reuse. Wordsplice is independent of YouGlish and YouTube.
